## Full description (4000 chars max)

```
BlockRush is a relaxing block puzzle game with a bold dark neon look. Drag pieces onto an 8×8 grid, complete full rows or columns, and watch them burst with glow and particles.

If you enjoy Block Blast–style puzzles but want something that feels sharper and more modern, BlockRush is built for you.

HOW TO PLAY
• You get three block shapes at a time — place any of them on the grid
• Fill an entire row or column to clear it and score bonus points
• When all three pieces are used, a fresh set appears
• The run ends when no piece can fit — how high can you score?

WHY PLAYERS LOVE IT
• No timer — play at your own pace
• Smooth drag-and-drop with ghost preview
• Neon clear effects, haptics, and satisfying sound
• Works offline — great for commutes and travel
• Save and resume your game anytime

FEATURES
• Classic endless mode on an 8×8 grid
• Local high score — beat your personal best
• Optional rewarded continue after game over
• Remove Ads purchase to hide banner and interstitial ads

Perfect for quick sessions or longer chill runs. Easy to learn, hard to put down.

Download BlockRush and light up the grid.
```
