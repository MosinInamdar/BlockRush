Overview

BlockRush is a casual puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app processes, why, and your choices. We designed the game to work offline and to collect only what is needed for ads, analytics, and purchases.

Data we collect

Data stored on your device (local only)

  • Game progress (grid, score, current pieces) — saved locally so you can resume
  • Best score and settings (e.g. sound on/off)
  • Remove Ads purchase flag (so ads stay hidden after purchase)

This data stays on your device unless you uninstall the app or clear app storage.

Data collected by third-party services

When you use the app, these partners may collect data according to their own policies:

Service Purpose Provider
Google AdMob Banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads Google LLC
Firebase Analytics App usage events (e.g. game start, game over) Google LLC
Google Play Billing In-app purchase (Remove Ads) on Android Google LLC
Apple In-App Purchase In-app purchase (Remove Ads) on iOS Apple Inc.

AdMob and Firebase may collect device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic data as described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

How we use data

  • Operate gameplay and save your progress
  • Show ads (unless you purchased Remove Ads for banner/interstitial)
  • Measure app performance and improve the game
  • Process in-app purchases and restore purchases

Children

BlockRush is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Your choices

  • Sound: Toggle sound effects in Settings
  • Ads: Purchase Remove Ads to hide banner and interstitial ads (rewarded ads remain optional)
  • Analytics: Limited opt-out may be available via device settings (e.g. Android “Opt out of Ads Personalization”); ad serving may still occur
  • Delete local data: Uninstall the app or clear storage in your device settings (Android: Settings → Apps → BlockRush; iOS: Settings → BlockRush → Offload/Delete App)

Data retention

  • Local game data: until you delete the app or clear data
  • Analytics and ad data: retained per Google’s policies

Security

We use industry-standard practices through Google’s SDKs. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

International users

The app is available in multiple countries. Data may be processed in countries where Google operates servers.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
